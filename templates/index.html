What do these metrics mean?
Symmetry Fidelity Score (SFS)
A composite score using accuracy (35%), SSIM (25%), recall (15%), 100% minus false positive rate (15%), and IoU (10%), with a penalty for background changes. The desktop app uses 95% as a display threshold; it is not a validated guarantee.
Accuracy
A weighted score: 60% of masked pixels changed during reconstruction plus 40% of unmasked pixels preserved.
Recall
Of all pixels in the detected mask, the percentage also changed by reconstruction.
False positive rate
Of pixels outside the detected mask, the percentage changed by reconstruction.
Intersection over Union (IoU)
Overlap between the detected mask and changed pixels, divided by their combined area.
Structural Similarity (SSIM)
Structural similarity between the input and reconstruction in regions outside the detected mask.

These are pipeline-derived measurements, not comparisons against independently labeled ground-truth damage. Recall, false positive rate, and IoU compare the detected mask with pixels changed by reconstruction. If no defects are detected, some mask metrics return default values and should not be interpreted as validated detection accuracy.
